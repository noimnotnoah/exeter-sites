Anvil & Pine.

Furniture made in Honiton.

James Hagley makes furniture. Oak, ash, walnut — solid timber, nothing flat-pack. Dining tables, shelving, bed frames. Each piece is drawn, cut, jointed, and finished by hand in a workshop at his home in Honiton.

There is no catalogue. No two pieces are alike, because every commission begins with a conversation — not a selection from a brochure. James listens to how you’ll use the piece, where it will live, what you want it to feel like. Then he designs around that.

Twenty years of carpentry, the last five working for himself. No employees, no showroom — just one man, a bench, and the timber. That is the whole operation.

If you need a table that seats eight, shelves that fit an awkward alcove, or a bed frame built to last longer than its owner, call or email. James works from his home in Honiton (EX14) and delivers across the South West.
