East Devon. Home visits.

A standard tuning is £65. I cover Exmouth to Axminster to Honiton, and everywhere in between.

“I come to you.”

Most of my work is done in people's own homes. I also keep a small workshop in Honiton for repairs, but 90% of what I do happens at your piano, in your front room.
